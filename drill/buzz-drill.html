How to read this chart

The horizontal axis shows how much of the question has been read. The vertical axis shows the share of all attempts you got right by that point. BPA (buzz point AUC) is the shaded area under the curve: your conversion at every 1% of the question, added up, so 0 to 100. A correct buzz halfway through adds half as much as one at the start. A correct answer at the end still counts toward conversion.

How this works, and how far to trust it

Each player gets a profile per category: when they tend to buzz, how often they're right, how often they neg, and how many bonus parts they convert. Thin categories are pulled toward the player's overall profile. On a question a player actually heard, their real buzz is used instead.

A team takes each question with its earliest buzz (or earliest correct buzz under best case), and a bonus part if any player would get it. That earliest buzz is played against every real room that heard the question (with this set's quizbowlstats buzzpoints imported), so the result is points per game against real teams. Without that data, the team plays uncontested.

Trust the ranking more than the exact numbers. Players are treated as independent, but teammates often know the same famous answers, so coverage is somewhat overstated. Profiles from a few dozen questions per category are noisy, and drilling alone isn't game conditions.
