A short guide

Find and read

StockFisher finds recurring clues for a person, work, place, or concept. Click any clue card to open the full source alongside your results.

Understand the numbers

Question counts tell you how often a clue appears on this answer. Specificity compares target-associated units found by the profile with all matching units in the index. Ranking balances frequency and association.

Keep useful clues

Select clues with their checkboxes, then use Export CSV. Your browser saves the file with the answer, source, and filters. Check a draft checks a proposed clue for answer words and supporting passages.

Keyboard: Ctrl+K or ⌘K focuses search. Escape closes the reading panel or dialog.
